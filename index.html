Why Their Legacy Matters

🔭 Past Discoveries

Early rovers and landers taught us how to explore other worlds, what to look for, and how to survive in harsh environments.

🚀 Future Missions

Every failure and success from abandoned equipment helps engineers design better robots for tomorrow's missions.

🌍 Human Exploration

Robotic missions prepare the way for humans to safely explore and live on the Moon and Mars.

💡 Scientific Knowledge

These machines discovered water, analyzed soil, found signs of past life, and expanded our understanding of the universe.
